Welcome to the Padel League App

This guide explains how to use the Purley Sports Club Padel League app — from signing in and checking the table to recording match results. Everything runs in your browser, no app download needed.

1

🔐 Signing In

The app requires an account. Go to app.davepadel.com and you'll land on the sign-in page.

Already have an account?

Enter your username and password on the Sign In tab and tap Sign In.

New to the app?

Tap Create Account, choose a username and password (at least 6 characters), and you're in.

→

Your session stays active for 7 days — you won't need to sign in every visit.

→

Forgotten your password? Contact the league administrator — they can reset it from the admin panel.

→

To change your password once logged in, use the Change Password button in the top-right of the app.

2

Choosing Your Division

Once signed in you'll see all the available divisions across the top. Tap the one you want to view.

Premier League Championship Ladies Division 1

The selected division is highlighted in blue. Everything below — the table, fixtures and outstanding games — belongs to that division.

3

📊 The League Table

The Table tab shows the current standings, ordered by Total Points (highest first). If teams are level on points, the team with the better set difference is ranked higher.

Row colours explained

Green — top of the table (promotion / league leaders)

Red — bottom of the table (danger zone)

White — mid-table

How points are scored

Each match is played over up to 3 sets. Each set won earns 1 point — so a team winning 2 sets to 1 earns 2 points and the losing team earns 1. Points accumulate across all matches to build the table.

4

📅 Fixtures

The Fixtures tab lists every match in the division. Completed matches show the set-by-set result and winner. Unplayed matches show a Record Result button.

Team Alpha vs Team Beta

Team Gamma vs Team Delta

Made a mistake? Use the grey Edit Score button on any completed fixture to correct it — the table updates automatically.

5

6

⏳ Outstanding Games

The Outstanding tab shows only the matches that haven't been played yet. The number in brackets updates as results come in. Use it as a quick checklist of what still needs recording.

7

Managing Teams

These controls are in the Table tab and are for the league administrator.

Add Team

Adds a new team to the selected division.

✏️ Rename (on each row)

Corrects a team name without touching their results or stats.

🗑️ Delete (on each row)

Removes a team and all their fixtures. You'll be asked to confirm first.

Reset Season

Clears all fixtures and resets every team's stats to zero. Team names are kept so you don't need to re-enter them for a new season.

8

Your Account

Your username is shown in the top-right of the app. Two buttons sit alongside it:

Change Password

Enter your current password, then your new one twice. Takes effect immediately.

Sign Out

Ends your session. You'll be taken back to the sign-in page.

Forgotten your password? You won't be able to log in to change it yourself. Contact the league administrator — they can reset it for you from the admin panel at app.davepadel.com/admin.html.

9

Quick Tips

→

The table updates instantly every time a score is saved — no need to refresh.

→

Fixtures are generated in a round-robin format — every team plays every other team once.

→

If a score was entered incorrectly, use Edit Score on the fixture — the table corrects itself automatically.

→

The app works on mobile — open it in your phone's browser at app.davepadel.com.

→

Your session lasts 7 days — you won't need to sign in on every visit from the same device.
